Adding BackpayRx as your Third-Party Support Entity

About five minutes in the MTF Data Module.

  1. Log in to the MTF Data Module with your pharmacy's account.
  2. Open your Dispensing Entity Enrollment Questionnaire and go to the Third-Party Support Entity section.
  3. Enter our name exactly as The Simple Thread LLC. That's the company behind BackpayRx, and it has to match for MTF to link us.
  4. For the function, choose Remittance support only. Don't choose payment support, so your refunds keep going straight to your own bank.
  5. Save and submit. MTF emails you a confirmation, and we'll email you once we see the link.

Menu names in the Data Module may differ slightly. If you get stuck, email hello@backpayrx.com and we'll walk you through it.

Questions pharmacy owners ask

Where do I find these files?

Log in to the Medicare Transaction Facilitator Data Module, the same account you enrolled in for the negotiated-price program. Download the Claims Tracking Report as a CSV and the 835 remittance files for the months you want checked. For the optional dispensing report, run a report in your pharmacy software for fills of the negotiated drugs with the Rx number, fill date and NDC.

Is my patient data safe?

For the free check, your files never leave your computer. The page reads them in your browser, and its security policy blocks it from sending anything over the internet. If you sign up for recovery, we work under a signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement, access your remittance data through MTF only, and keep it off email and cloud storage.

Are you part of Medicare or CMS?

No. BackpayRx is a private company and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for CMS, Medicare, or any drug manufacturer.

How accurate is "likely owed"?

It's an estimate from your own files. We compare each claim's expected refund (the Standard Default Refund Amount in your MTF data) with what was actually paid. Some short payments are legitimate, for example when a manufacturer uses your contract price or a 340B adjustment applies. Those are listed separately and aren't counted as owed.

What does recovery cost?

20% of the refund money we actually recover, invoiced monthly after it lands in your account, with a claim-by-claim breakdown. Pay by bank transfer, card, or check. If we recover nothing, you pay nothing. There are no setup fees or contracts, and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

Do you ever hold my money?

No. You link us for remittance support only, so refunds keep going directly to your bank. We invoice our fee separately.
