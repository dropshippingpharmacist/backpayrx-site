How to add us as your Third‑Party Support Entity

  1. Log in to the MTF Data Module with your pharmacy's account.
  2. Open your Dispensing Entity Enrollment Questionnaire and go to the Third‑Party Support Entity section.
  3. Select or enter our TPSE name exactly as: Simple Thread LLC (BackpayRx is our brand name). It must match exactly for MTF to link us.
  4. For the function, choose Remittance support only. Do not choose payment support: your refunds should keep going straight to your own bank account.
  5. Save and submit. MTF emails you a confirmation, and once we see the link we'll email you to confirm.

Menu names in the MTF Data Module may differ slightly. If you get stuck, email hello@backpayrx.com and we'll walk you through it.

Questions

Where do I find these files?

Log in to the Medicare Transaction Facilitator Data Module, the same account you enrolled in for the negotiated‑price program. Download the Claims Tracking Report as a CSV and the 835 remittance files for the months you want checked. For the optional dispensing report, run a report in your pharmacy software for fills of the negotiated drugs with the Rx number, fill date and NDC.

Is my patient data safe?

For the free check, your files never leave your computer. The page reads them in your browser, and its security policy blocks it from sending anything over the internet. If you sign up for done‑for‑you recovery, we work under a signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement, access your remittance data through MTF only, and keep it off email and cloud storage.

Are you part of Medicare or CMS?

No. BackpayRx is a private company and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for CMS, Medicare, or any drug manufacturer.

How accurate is "likely owed"?

It's an estimate from your own files. We compare each claim's expected refund (the Standard Default Refund Amount from MTF data) with what was actually paid. Some short payments are legitimate, for example when a manufacturer uses your contract price or a 340B adjustment applies, and we list those separately and don't count them as owed.

What does recovery cost?

20% of the refund money we actually recover, invoiced after it lands in your account. If we recover nothing, you pay nothing. There are no setup fees or contracts, and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

Do you ever hold my money?

No. You link us for remittance support only, so refunds keep going directly to your bank. We invoice our fee separately.
